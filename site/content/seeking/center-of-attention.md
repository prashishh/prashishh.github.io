My *center of attention* in 2025 was [the self](/seeking/), and in 2026 it has been [AI](/ai/).

Last year, I read the *Upanishads*, [meditated](/seeking/the-space-between/), [practiced yoga](/seeking/default-mode/), and tried to understand [what it means to know yourself](/seeking/was-that-true-or-is-this-true/). Most of my attention pointed inward.

This year, it has shifted outward. I have been reading books on artificial intelligence and technology, listening to podcasts, and following how fast the [software industry is changing](/fragments/software-developments-ground-shift/) and how it is [disrupting almost every market](/fragments/infinite-software/). I am trying to understand not only what AI can do, but [who gets to use it](/ai/ais-widening-access-gap/), and what it is changing about how we [work](/ai/shared-intelligence-layer/), [create](/ai/software-for-one-powered-by-llms/), and [think](/ai/shared-intelligence-layer/).

My center of attention is whatever pulls most of my focus. For whatever reason, good or bad, 2026 is not what I expected in 2025. But I am curious to learn why it shifted, whether it came from something in me or from everything moving so fast around me.
